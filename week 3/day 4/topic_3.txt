Gradient Descent is a way to teach a computer to get better at something by correcting its own mistakes.



Think of it as a game of "Hot or Cold."Imagine you are trying to guess a secret number between 1 and 100.You guess 50.The computer says: 
"Too high! (Cold)"Because it said "too high," you know your next guess needs to be lower. You guess 40.

The computer says: "Closer! (Warmer)"Gradient Descent is just a mathematical version of that game.




The Goal: A computer makes a guess (like predicting a house price).The Loss: The computer calculates how wrong its guess was. 

This mistake is called the Loss or Error.The Gradient: The computer looks at its mistake and calculates a number called the Gradient. 
The gradient is just a compass pointing toward the mistakes.


The Descent: The computer takes a step in the opposite direction of that compass to make a smaller mistake next time.
