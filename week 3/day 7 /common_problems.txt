What is a Gradient?A gradient measures how much a value changes over a specific distance.

In simple terms, it is a measure of steepness. In everyday life, you encounter gradients when looking at the slope of a hill or a wheelchair ramp.

What is a Gradient Vector?When you move from a flat 2D line to a 3D surface (or higher dimensions), 

the gradient becomes a gradient vector.Instead of just one number, it is a list of numbers (a vector)

 that contains the slope for every single direction you can move. It is represented by the symbol \(\nabla \)
