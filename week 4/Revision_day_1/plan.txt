1. Basic probability
   favourable outcomes / total outcomes
   Example mistake: rolling a 4 ≠ 4/6, it is 1/6

2. Independent events
   Heads AND Heads
   multiply the two probabilities

3. Bayes intuition
   starting probability
   + new evidence
   → updated probability

4. Uniform vs normal distribution
   Uniform = every outcome equally likely
   Normal = values near the mean are more common

5. Normal distribution NumPy syntax
   rng.normal(loc=..., scale=..., size=...)

6. Simulation counting
   np.sum(rolls == 6)

7. Observed probability from simulation
   count / total
   then × 100 for percentage

8. Standard error
   SE = standard deviation / √sample size
   larger sample → smaller SE
   understand WHY S/20 is smaller than S/5
