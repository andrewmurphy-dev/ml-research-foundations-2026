# Day 4 — Sampling Variability, Standard Error, Confidence Intervals

## Topic 1 — Sample

* Population = the full group
* Sample = a smaller group taken from the population
* We use samples because we usually cannot measure everything

## Topic 2 — Sampling Variability

* Different samples can give different results
* Sample means can change from sample to sample
* Small samples usually give more unstable results

## Topic 3 — Standard Error

* Standard deviation = spread of individual values
* Standard error = how much the sample mean tends to change between samples

## Topic 4 — Confidence Interval

* A confidence interval gives a range around an estimate
* Example:

```text
mean = 50
confidence interval = 47 to 53
```

* It helps show the uncertainty around the estimate

## Topic 5 — NumPy Simulation

```text
take sample
↓
calculate mean
↓
take another sample
↓
calculate mean
↓
compare results
```

## Main Idea

```text
A result from a sample is an estimate.

Different samples can give different answers.
```
