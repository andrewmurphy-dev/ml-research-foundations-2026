#effect size 


Effect size tells us:

how big the difference or relationship actually is.

Example:

Group A average score = 80
Group B average score = 81

There is a difference:

81 - 80 = 1

But that difference is tiny.

Now:

Group A average score = 80
Group B average score = 95

Difference:

95 - 80 = 15

That is a much larger effect.

So the idea is:

statistical result
→ tells us there IS a difference


effect size
→ tells us HOW BIG the difference is

In ML, this matters because a model improvement like:

accuracy: 90.0% → 90.1%

might technically be an improvement, but the effect is tiny.
