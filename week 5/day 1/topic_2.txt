#what is Baseline?

#A baseline is a very simple prediction we use as a minimum standard.

#minimum standard, I mean:

#the basic level of performance your real model should at least beat.



By **minimum standard**, I mean:

> the **basic level of performance your real model should at least beat**.




Example:


baseline prediction:
always predict 50


Suppose that simple rule gets an average error of:


20


Then your ML model should ideally get:


error < 20


If your ML model gets an error of:


25


then it is **worse than the simple baseline**.

So think of baseline as:

> **the bar to beat**

If your model cannot beat that bar, the extra complexity probably is not helping.
