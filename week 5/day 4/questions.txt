Two small word fixes so your mental picture stays accurate:

1. Nothing gets “eaten.” No rows disappear. The 50% is set aside as the test set, and the other 50% becomes the train set. Add them back together and you always get your original 10.

2. Call it “rows,” not “x axis.” “x axis” gets confusing because your data is also called X. In NumPy terms:

rows = axis 0, the first number in the shape: (10, 2)
columns = axis 1, the second number: (10, 2)

So train_test_split divides axis 0 (rows) and never touches axis 1 (columns).
