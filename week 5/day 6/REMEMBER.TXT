A smaller number minus a larger one is always negative:

regression is predictiing a number 

classification is predicting a category 

**Regression** = predicting a **number**.

- Examples: house price, temperature, exam score
- Measured with **MSE**: how far off the guesses are

**Classification** = predicting a **category**.

- Examples: species 0/1/2, spam or not
- Measured with **accuracy** and the **confusion matrix**
