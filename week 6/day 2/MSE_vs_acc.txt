how to know when to use MSE, vs accuracy 


Predicting a category → accuracy (plus the confusion matrix)
Predicting a number → MSE



Iris: categories → accuracy. Each guess is simply right or wrong. Species 1 is either species 1 or it isn’t. So the natural question is “what fraction did it get right?”, and that’s accuracy.

Diabetes: numbers → MSE. The target is a number like 151 or 75. A guess will almost never be exactly right, but some are close and some are way off:

real 152, guess 150 → great guess
real 152, guess 60 → terrible guess
