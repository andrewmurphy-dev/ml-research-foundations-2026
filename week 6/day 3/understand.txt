## .fit is different for each model

### KNN
- .fit = just SAVES the training data
- No maths during fit
- The real work happens at .predict (measuring distances)

### LinearRegression
- .fit = does REAL MATHS
- Finds the best weight for each feature + a bias
- Goal: make X @ w + b as close as possible to y_train
- Same idea as Week 3 (weights, dot product)
- .predict is fast: just X @ w + b

### StandardScaler
- .fit = learns each column's mean and std

### Same word, same idea
- .fit always = learn from the TRAINING data
- What "learn" means depends on the tool

## LinearRegression formula
prediction = w1·x1 + w2·x2 + ... + w10·x10 + b

## What it learned
- model.coef_ → the weights (one per feature)
  - big positive → pushes prediction UP
  - big negative → pushes prediction DOWN
- model.intercept_ → the bias b (starting point)
- Trailing _ = something the model LEARNED during .fit
